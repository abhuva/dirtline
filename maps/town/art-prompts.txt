BUILT-IN IMAGE GENERATION PROMPTS
=================================

Exterior
--------
Use case: production-game-asset source, reduced by the asset pipeline to a
256x256, 15-colour Game Boy Advance regular background.

Use the three attached town images as broad style and composition references
only. Create an original Dustline location rather than copying their layouts.
Draw a lively, modern wasteland outpost where civilization has transformed but
has not collapsed: maintained homes and workshops, an active market, water and
solar infrastructure, planters, lamps, repaired surfaces, and restrained wear.
Use a strict 90-degree orthographic top-down view with no isometric tilt. Build
the scene on a modular 16-pixel-like tile rhythm with simple rectangular building
footprints and readable axis-aligned collision edges. Put a broad north-south
street through the center, an open gate centered on the south edge, a mechanic
garage with a centered entrance at the north, a dispatch building to the west,
and a matching race office on the east,
and smaller mixed-use buildings around the street. Keep all walking routes clear
for a small overhead player sprite. Polished modern pixel art with crisp clusters,
muted teal, concrete blue, warm ochre, rust, and sun-faded accents. No characters,
UI, labels, text, logos, perspective walls, irregular cliff silhouettes, total
ruin, black border, or watermark.

Garage interior
---------------
Use the completed exterior as the palette and visual-style reference. Create an
original active mechanic garage in the same strict 90-degree orthographic
top-down pixel-art style. Use modular rectangular architecture and fixtures: a
wide open central aisle, a long mechanic counter against the north wall, a
centered exit at the south, organized tool walls, workbenches, tires, parts,
two service bays, and one car chassis parked off the walking route. Make this a
working community garage rather than an abandoned ruin. Preserve obvious,
axis-aligned collision footprints and enough clearance for the overhead player.
No characters, dialogue, UI, labels, text, logos, perspective tilt, irregular
walls, black border, or watermark.

Player sprite sheet - final corrected pass
------------------------------------------
Create a production pixel-art walking sheet for the town player, matching the
town palette. Show the same distinctive wasteland courier/mechanic in a teal
jacket, orange scarf, practical trousers, boots, and compact backpack. The camera
must be a steep 75-80 degree top-down view: the crown of the hair, shoulders,
backpack, and boots are the dominant readable forms, with strong foreshortening
and very little profile view. The result should feel more zoomed out than the
environment while retaining a high-quality silhouette and crisp pixel clusters.
Arrange exactly twelve separate, consistently aligned sprites in a clean 3-column
by 4-row sheet: rows are south, west, east, north; columns are idle, left step,
right step. Keep one consistent character identity and proportions in every
frame. Transparent background, no floor shadows baked into the source, labels,
text, UI, extra objects, logos, border, or watermark.

The first player generation was edited with the same request while preserving
the sheet layout, clothing, palette, and character identity; the edit specifically
corrected the overly side-on camera and foreshortening.
