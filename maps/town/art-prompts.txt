Exterior
--------
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: source artwork for a Game Boy Advance top-down town background
Input image: Image 1 is a style reference only; create original artwork and do not copy its exact layout or objects.
Primary request: Create a square, straight top-down pixel-art desert settlement map that can be explored on foot. It needs a clear entrance centered on the south edge, a broad connected sandy walking area, a prominent mechanic garage along the north side with one clearly readable centered doorway, a water tower, scrap piles, cacti, rocks, fencing, and two smaller closed buildings.
Style/medium: polished 16-bit-era pixel art, warm canyon desert, rugged post-apocalyptic roadside settlement, crisp deliberate pixel clusters and readable silhouettes.
Composition/framing: strict orthographic top-down game map, no perspective tilt, no characters, no UI, no labels, no text. Keep the center and paths open enough for a 16x24 player sprite. Solid cliffs and buildings should form obvious boundaries. The garage doorway must be reachable from the south entrance.
Lighting/mood: bright warm daylight with ochre sand, rusted metal, deep brown shadows.
Constraints: seamless visual edges within the square; all content inside frame; no vehicles blocking paths; no logos, words, watermark, interface, tile grid, or black borders. Favor repeated ground texture over unique noisy detail so it remains readable after reduction to 256x256 and 16 colors.
